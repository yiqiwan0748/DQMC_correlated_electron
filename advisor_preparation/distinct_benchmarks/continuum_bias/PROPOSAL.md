## Why even time-step fits apply here

This derivation applies to the fully summed interacting Hubbard transfer operator. It does not assert the same single similarity transformation for every fluctuating auxiliary-field configuration.

Let `V=4*sum_i(nup_i−1/2)(ndown_i−1/2)` and `T(h)=exp(−hV)exp(−hK)`. The Hubbard–Stratonovich sum is exact at fixed h, so this is the ensemble transfer operator represented by the native sampler. Put

`D(h)=exp(−hV/2)` and `S(h)=D(h)exp(−hK)D(h)`.

Then `T(h)=D(h)S(h)D(h)^−1`, so after M=beta/h slices and trace normalization, `rho_T=D rho_S D^−1`. S is a real symmetric positive transfer matrix. Its symmetric product has global error O(h²); equivalently, its effective Hamiltonian has an even expansion in h near0.

Because V is a sum of onsite even operators, `D=D_A tensor D_B` in the spatial fermionic factorization. Cyclicity of the partial trace gives

`rho_T,A=D_A rho_S,A D_A^−1`.

Therefore `Tr(rho_T,A^2)=Tr(rho_S,A^2)` exactly. The non-Hermitian asymmetric reduced operator has the same spectrum and purity as this positive symmetric reduced state. This proves an O(h²) continuum expansion for this cut's S2; a similarity transform generated by nonlocal hopping would not provide that cut-local argument.

For energy, `Tr(Hphys rho_T)=Tr(D^−1 Hphys D rho_S)`. Expanding the transformed observable yields `Hphys+(h/2)[V,Hphys]+(h²/8)[V,[V,Hphys]]+...`. Hphys and V are real symmetric; odd nested commutators are real antisymmetric, whose trace against symmetric rho_S vanishes. Thus no odd leading term survives, and physical internal energy also has an even expansion with O(h²) leading bias. The chemical-potential number operator commutes with D, so restoring physical energy does not alter this conclusion. This argument is specific to the real model and local split; no complex-flux or generic-nonlocal-interaction claim is made.

[Grover's primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.1486) supplies the auxiliary-field Gaussian decomposition of thermal reduced states and the Renyi estimator. The cut-local similarity and odd-commutator arguments above are explicit derivations for this protocol, not claims copied from that paper.

The same independent matrices also produce exact finite-h transfer operators for diagnostics. Before trusting a quadratic extrapolation, verify the full-state similarity identity, cut-reduced similarity/purity identity, normalization, reality/positivity of purity and symmetric-transfer positivity, with absolute matrix/scalar tolerances to be fixed at1e−10. Compare direct integer matrix powers with the equivalent symmetric diagonalization. Do not symmetrize rho_T before partial tracing: that would change its native observable. A diagnostic identity failure is UNRESOLVED, rather than permission to change the oracle convention after execution.

Every MC time point should additionally be compared with its own independent exact finite-h energy/S2, while the scientific endpoint remains the continuum trace. These diagnostic comparisons cannot complete X01 on their own.

## Proposed precision stages and independent bin analysis

Freeze three production stages at16384,65536,262144 measured update rounds per chain/node; use2048 burn-in rounds and64 retained bins for every chain. Each stage restarts with a new, preregistered namespace of seeds and uses the full four-step grid. The stages are not pooled. A separate timing pilot is excluded from every fit and interval. All root seeds and all derived native replica seeds must be generated and checked for collisions before that pilot.

One chain bundle contains a separate native scalar-energy stream and four native entropy-node streams; energy seeds are independent of the entropy-node seeds. Raw paired numerator/phase bins are retained. Within each entropy bundle, calculate the sum of four negative logarithms of adjacent ratios and apply joint delete-one-bin jackknife to that whole nonlinear functional. Removing bin b means removing it from every node; numerator and denominator from the same bin remain paired. This yields one bias-corrected S2 and its diagnostics per independent bundle. Ratios must stay finite and positive for the full and every leave-one-out sample.

For both observables, form64-bin and32-paired-bin jackknife errors, require every chain's relative error change at most0.5, split-Rhat at most1.05, estimated effective bins at least128 across the eight chains and at least16 in each chain, finite stabilization diagnostics and the positive-sign half-filled ensemble. These are rejection diagnostics, not proofs of equilibrium or exact confidence coverage. A frozen numerical-stability threshold still needs to be selected from the actual deltaG convention before implementation.

The eight independent chain bundles produce eight independent four-point vectors for each observable. Apply every fixed linear fit to each complete vector, then use the eight fitted intercepts or residual contrasts as the independent statistical units. This avoids estimating a data-dependent covariance/weight matrix from only four means. Use unweighted OLS with fixed design matrices; heteroscedasticity is handled by the empirical covariance of the independent bundle-level contrasts. No weights, fit window or polynomial order are selected after seeing the results.

## Proposed endpoint and adequacy gates

Here “quadratic” means quadratic in h: the primary full-grid fit is `y(h)=a+b*h²`. Require a leading-order fit on the three finest points after dropping the coarsest, plus a mandatory full-grid stability fit `y(h)=a+b*h²+c*h⁴`. The extra fit is a fixed check, never a replacement selected because it gives a more favorable endpoint.

Suggested final equivalence margins are0.02 for total physical energy and0.01 for S2. For each observable require all three extrapolated intercept confidence intervals to lie entirely inside its continuum margin. Require the primary/drop-coarsest and primary/quartic intercept-difference intervals to lie inside half those margins (0.01 and0.005). Require all four primary-fit residual intervals to lie inside the full respective margins. Also require all four MC-minus-exact-finite-h intervals to lie inside the respective full margins.

This gives26 predeclared scalar confidence intervals per stage:6 endpoint intervals,4 fit-stability differences,8 full-grid residuals and8 finite-h diagnostic differences. Use nominal familywise alpha0.01 over at most three stages, with two-sided Student-t7 critical value at per-interval alpha `0.01/(3*26)`. Both observables must pass all applicable diagnostics, precision and adequacy conditions in the same stage. Stop after the first wholly passing stage; spend no unallocated extra looks. Retain failed/unresolved earlier stages and never replace an individual chain or time point. A confidence interval wholly outside an equivalence region is FAIL; insufficient precision, failed equilibration/numerical eligibility, or unresolved fit adequacy is UNRESOLVED. Exhausting all stages does not justify widening a margin.

These Student intervals use independent-chain asymptotics and approximate normality; Bonferroni controls nominal multiplicity conditional on that interval approximation. The earlier X06 coverage study calibrated a95% energy interval in another fixture, not these much more stringent tails or nonlinear-entropy endpoints. No exact or universal coverage guarantee should be claimed.
