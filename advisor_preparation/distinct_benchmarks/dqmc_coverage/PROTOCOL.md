X06 asks whether the actual DQMC uncertainty interval covers an independently known finite-time-step energy at its nominal frequency. It is one distinct calibration question. Its 400 repetitions, eight chains, bins, quality gates, and statistical comparisons are not additional benchmark ideas. No scientific simulation has been run at protocol freeze.

The fixture is a four-site periodic Hubbard chain with t=1, U=2, centered chemical potential 0, beta=1, dtau=0.1, grand canonical ensemble and stabilization interval 2. The target observable is the *uncentered total energy* (kinetic plus U times total double occupancy). The oracle is the independently constructed QuSpin finite-step trace for `[exp(-dtau V)exp(-dtau K)]^10`, case QT-01, energy −1.4650726727669701. Its existing immutable oracle TOML SHA256 is recorded in the manifest. This avoids confusing a Trotter bias with sampling-interval miscalibration. The oracle does not depend on a DQMC result. The actual corrected HubbardRenyiDQMC initializer and zero-lambda update routines generate every target sample; this adapter changes no Markov kernel.

Every repetition uses eight independent, freshly initialized chains, 512 burn sweeps and 1024 measured sweeps per chain. Measurements are accumulated into 64 paired sign-weighted numerator and denominator bins. The energy, paired delete-one-bin ratio jackknife bias correction, adjacent-bin merge, initial-positive-autocorrelation effective-bin estimate, and split R-hat are the same formulas used in the existing production validation consumer. The repetition estimate is the mean of the eight jackknife-corrected chain means, with standard error equal to their sample standard deviation divided by sqrt(8). There is no fitted correction or rescaling of the estimator or uncertainty.

The nominal 95% interval uses the Student-t distribution with seven degrees of freedom, critical value 2.3646242515927844. This calibrates this explicitly named nominal interval and estimator on this one fixture. It does not calibrate the earlier campaign's extreme Bonferroni tail, all model regimes, all observables, or a mathematical confidence theorem for correlated Markov-chain estimates.

The four pilot repetitions use a separate seed namespace and are excluded from every final statistic. All 400 final repetitions and their 3200 chain seeds are fixed before the pilot. The first eight bytes of each labeled SHA256 digest, interpreted big endian and masked to 63 bits, determine the positive Julia integer seed; `seeds.toml` lists all final and pilot seeds. The two sets are disjoint and contain no duplicates. Repetitions may be divided into at most eight nonoverlapping fixed index ranges for ISAAC scheduling. The final aggregator requires exactly indices 1 through 400 once each, matching source/driver/manifest/oracle hashes, Julia 1.12.1, and one BLAS thread. No missing interval, failed quality gate, or non-covering repetition may be deleted or replaced.

For every repetition, split R-hat must be <=1.05, the maximum adjacent-block standard-error change across all eight chains must be <=0.5, estimated effective bins summed over chains must be >=128, and all reported values/intervals/sign denominators must be finite and valid. Any quality failure leaves the scientific result UNRESOLVED; the unconditional all-400 coverage remains reported when the intervals exist. This deliberately avoids reporting coverage after selecting only well-behaved repetitions. The quality diagnostics do not prove mixing.

All statistical gates are fixed before pilot execution:

- Exact two-sided binomial test of H0: coverage probability =0.95 at significance 0.01, using SciPy `binomtest`; p>=0.01 is required. A small p for excess coverage also fails the nominal calibration test.
- The 99% exact Clopper–Pearson interval for the coverage probability must lie wholly inside [0.90,0.995]. Non-rejection alone is insufficient for PASS. A binomial discrepancy with all quality gates passed is FAIL; insufficient equivalence precision is UNRESOLVED.
- Across the 400 independent repetition estimates, a 99% Student-t399 interval for mean energy bias must lie wholly inside [−0.02,+0.02] in total-energy units. A bias interval wholly beyond that margin is FAIL; overlap with the margin is UNRESOLVED.
- All three tests have their individually stated nominal levels; no familywise confidence claim across them or across the wider catalogue is made. The finite-repetition binomial interval assumes independent identically distributed repetitions; finite-chain Student intervals are the object being empirically calibrated, not an assumed exact sampling theorem.

The Python summary independently recomputes chain means, standard errors, merged-block errors, effective-bin estimates, split R-hat, all intervals, coverage indicators and quality decisions from the retained raw bins. It then applies the frozen tests and records every repetition's diagnostics. It uses the existing isolated QuSpin environment's SciPy; no new statistical package installation is needed. [SciPy's binomial test](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.binomtest.html), [beta quantiles](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.beta.html), and [Student-t quantiles](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.t.html) define the statistical API. The independent fermion reference follows the [QuSpin Hubbard conventions](https://quspin.github.io/QuSpin/examples/example6.html).

Runtime is initially unmeasured. Run only the four-repetition pilot first, retain its raw bins and wall/sampling times, and extrapolate the average sampling time to 400 repetitions (also report a conservative three-times estimate). Final sampling comprises 4,915,200 tiny sweeps. The fixed resource ceiling is one CPU and 3500 MB per shard, at most eight shards, with a 120-minute cap per shard. The parent operator chooses fixed shard boundaries after timing, without changing scientific settings. A cap or timeout yields incomplete/UNRESOLVED, never a reduced denominator or an automatic reroll. Pilot output is always PILOT_ONLY, never a completed X06 result.
